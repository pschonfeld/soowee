## Chef’s Tips

- **Keep the batter cold:** For the crispiest fish, keep the batter cold by placing it in the refrigerator for about 30 minutes before using. The cold batter will react better with the hot oil, resulting in a lighter, crispier coating.
- **Double fry for extra crispiness:** Double-fry the fish for a crispy finish. Fry the fish for the first time until it’s just cooked, then remove it and let it cool slightly. Fry it again at a slightly higher temperature for 2–3 minutes to achieve an extra crunchy texture.
- **Dry the fish:** Pat the cod fillets dry with paper towels before seasoning and battering. It helps the batter adhere better and results in a crisper coating.

## Serving Suggestions

- For an authentic British experience, serve the crispy fish fillets with the pea purée and a side of chips (French fries).
- Optionally, garnish with lemon wedges and a sprinkle of malt vinegar.

## Allergen Warnings

- **Gluten:** This recipe contains all-purpose flour and beer, which may contain gluten. If needed, substitute gluten-free flour and gluten-free beer.
- **Dairy:** The pea purée contains butter, which is unsuitable for those with dairy allergies or lactose intolerance. Replace with plant-based butter for a dairy-free option.
- **Alcohol:** The batter contains beer, which may not be suitable for individuals avoiding alcohol. If necessary, opt for a non-alcoholic beer.
- **Fish:** This recipe contains cod, which is a common allergen for those with fish allergies. Ensure no cross-contamination occurs during preparation.
- **Frying oil:** If using shared oil, ensure it hasn’t been used for frying other allergens, such as shellfish, to avoid cross-contamination.
