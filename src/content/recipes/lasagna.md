steps:
  - title: Prepare the cheeses
    text:
      - Tear {250 g} **mozzarella fior di latte** into small pieces and drain on kitchen paper for at least {timer:30m}, ideally overnight in the fridge.
      - tip: Start here. The mozzarella drains while the ragù cooks. Use fior di latte, not mozzarella di bufala, which releases too much water in the oven and turns the layers wet.
      - Grate {80 g} **Pecorino Romano** and set {30 g} aside for the top. Shred {100 g} **Provolone Piccante**.
      - tip: Parmigiano Reggiano can replace the Pecorino Romano. If only Provolone dolce is available, use it and add {20 g} more Pecorino Romano for bite.
  - title: Make the ragù
    text:
      - Heat {2 tbsp} **extra virgin olive oil** in a large heavy-bottomed pot over medium heat.
      - Add the **onion**, **carrot**, **celery** and a pinch of **salt**. Cook for {timer:5-10m} until softened and sweet.
      - tip: Do not let the vegetables brown. Salting them now draws out moisture so they sweat instead of frying.
      - Add {500 g} **ground beef** and {500 g} **ground pork** and season generously with **salt**.
      - Leave the meat untouched for {timer:2-3m} so it takes colour, then break it down with a wooden spoon and cook until no pink remains.
      - Pour in {240 ml} **red wine** and simmer, stirring occasionally, until the smell of alcohol is gone and the liquid has almost evaporated.
      - Stir in {1.4 L} **passata**. Reduce the heat to medium-low, cover the pot and simmer for {wait:1h}, stirring occasionally.
      - Remove the lid, add {240 ml} **water** and simmer uncovered on low heat for {wait:1h} more, stirring occasionally.
      - tip: The ragù should stay loose and spoonable; add a splash of water if it thickens. Fresh pasta sheets take their moisture from the sauce.
      - Taste and adjust with **salt** and **black pepper**.
      - tip: Keep the seasoning slightly under. The Pecorino Romano brings salt of its own.
  - title: Make the béchamel
    text:
      - While the ragù finishes, melt {40 g} **butter** in a saucepan over low heat.
      - Whisk in {40 g} **flour** and cook for {timer:1-2m} until the raw flour smell is gone, without letting it colour.
      - Add {400 ml} **milk** gradually, a little at a time, whisking constantly.
      - Cook over low heat, whisking, until the sauce thickens and coats the back of a spoon.
      - Season with a pinch of **salt** and **nutmeg**, remove from the heat and keep warm.
  - title: Assemble the lasagna
    heat: Preheat the **oven** to **180 °C**, conventional top and bottom heat, with a rack in the lower-middle position.
    text:
      - tip: Do not use the fan setting. It dries the surface and edges before the centre is hot.
      - Grease the baking dish with **extra virgin olive oil** using a pastry brush.
      - Trim {500 g} **egg pasta sheets** to fit the dish.
      - tip: There is no need to boil fresh sheets. They cook in the sauce.
      - Spread a thin layer of ragù over the bottom of the dish.
      - Place a layer of pasta sheets over the ragù.
      - Cover with a thin layer of ragù, then scatter over a share of the **Pecorino Romano**, **Provolone** and **mozzarella**, divided evenly across the layers.
      - Spoon over a thin veil of **béchamel** and spread lightly so it fills the gaps between the cheese.
      - tip: Keep every layer thin and even. Too much sauce and the lasagna loses shape and slides apart when served. Hold back a little ragù and about a quarter of the béchamel for the top.
      - Repeat the layering until you have 5–6 layers, finishing with a sheet of pasta.
      - Spread a thin layer of ragù over the top sheet, then the remaining béchamel from edge to edge so no pasta is exposed.
  - title: Bake
    text:
      - Cover the dish with foil, without letting it touch the surface, and bake for {wait:20m}.
      - Remove the foil, sprinkle with the reserved {30 g} **Pecorino Romano** and bake uncovered for {wait:15-20m} until the top is golden and the edges are bubbling.
      - Rest for {wait:15-20m} before slicing.
      - tip: Resting lets the béchamel and cheese set so the slices hold their shape.

## Chef’s Notes

- **Make ahead:** Assemble a day ahead and bake from the fridge, adding 15 minutes to the covered bake. The lasagna slices and reheats better the next day.
- **To serve:** Pair with a crisp green salad and a robust Italian red such as Chianti Classico or Aglianico.

## Allergens

- **Milk:** butter, milk, mozzarella, Pecorino Romano, Provolone.
- **Gluten:** flour and pasta sheets.
- **Egg:** fresh egg pasta sheets.
- **Celery:** in the ragù.
- **Sulphites:** red wine.
