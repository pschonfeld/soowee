directions:
  - title: Whip the cream
    steps:
      - Pour {200 ml whipping cream}, straight from the fridge, into a chilled bowl and whip on medium speed for {timer:2-4m}. Stop as soon as it thickens and the peak on the lifted beaters holds but bends over. Put the whipped cream in the fridge.
      - tip: Don’t whip it stiff. Stiff cream gets overworked as it is folded into the dense quark, turns grainy and weeps. Cream with 31 % fat whips less easily, so keep it as cold as you can.
  - title: Mix the quark with cocoa
    steps:
      - Put {500 g quark} in a second bowl, sift {2 tbsp cocoa powder} over it through a fine-mesh sieve and add {1 tsp vanilla extract}, a pinch of {salt} and {50 ml whipping cream}.
      - Beat with the same beaters, no need to wash them, for {timer:1-2m}, until smooth, free of curds and evenly brown.
      - tip: The cream dissolves the cocoa without lumps. Semi-skimmed quark is grainy and the grains would stay in the cream. For a perfectly smooth cream, blend the quark with a stick blender first.
  - title: Fold the cream together
    steps:
      - Stir a third of the whipped cream into the quark with the spatula to loosen it. Fold in the rest gently in two additions, until no pale streaks remain.
      - Spoon the cream into glasses and serve straight away or chilled.

## Chef’s Notes

- **Make ahead:** The cream can be made up to a day ahead. After an hour in the fridge it thickens and the cocoa comes through more.
- **Store:** Covered, it keeps 2 days in the fridge. If a little whey separates, just stir it back in.
- **Swap:** For a sweeter version without sugar, stir 1 ripe mashed banana into the quark. Adults can sweeten the cream with 1–2 tablespoons of icing sugar or honey. Don’t give honey to children under 1 year.
- **Serve:** With strawberries, raspberries or sliced banana.

## Allergens

- **Milk:** cream and quark.
