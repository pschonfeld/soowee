steps:
  - title: Make the coffee
    text:
      - Brew {400 ml} **strong coffee or espresso**, pour it into a shallow dish and let it cool completely while you make the cream.
  - title: Make the cream
    text:
      - Separate {6} **eggs**, putting the whites in one large, clean bowl and the yolks in another.
      - Add a pinch of **salt** to the whites and whisk until glossy and thick. When you lift the beaters, the peaks should hold, with the tips just bending over. Stop before they look dry or grainy.
      - tip: Whip the whites first. The same beaters can then go straight into the yolks without washing; the other way round, a trace of yolk fat would stop the whites from whipping.
      - Whisk the yolks with {6 tbsp} **sugar** for {timer:4-5m}, until pale, thick and creamy. The sugar should no longer feel gritty between your fingers, and the mixture should fall from the beaters in a ribbon that sits on the surface for a moment.
      - Add {750 g} **mascarpone**, straight from the fridge, and beat on low speed just until smooth.
      - tip: Stop as soon as the mascarpone is smooth. Overbeaten, it turns grainy and can split.
      - Stir a large spoonful of the whites into the cream to loosen it, then fold in the rest in two additions, cutting down through the middle and turning the bowl, until no streaks remain.
  - title: Assemble
    text:
      - Dip {18} **Savoiardi biscuits**, one at a time, 1–2 seconds a side in the cooled **coffee**, and lay them in a single, snug layer in the dish. The centre should still feel firm; it softens overnight.
      - tip: The biscuits decide the dish. Pick one that half of them cover in a single snug layer, and trim them to fit if needed.
      - Spread half of the cream over the biscuits and smooth it to the edges.
      - Dust with a thin, even layer of **cocoa powder** through a fine sieve and grate {10 g} **dark chocolate** over it.
      - Dip the remaining {18} **Savoiardi biscuits** in the **coffee** the same way, lay them on top and spread over the rest of the cream, smoothing the top.
      - Dust the top with an even layer of **cocoa powder**.
  - title: Chill and serve
    text:
      - Cover and refrigerate overnight, for at least {chill:8h}. It is at its best after 1–2 days.
      - Just before serving, dust the top with a little more **cocoa powder**. Slice and serve chilled, with a little **icing sugar** if you like.
      - tip: The cocoa on top darkens overnight as it absorbs moisture from the cream; the fresh dusting brings back the colour and the dry, bitter finish.
      - tip: The eggs are raw. For pregnant, elderly, very young or immunocompromised guests, use pasteurised eggs.

## Chef’s Notes

- **Make ahead:** Tiramisu needs the night in the fridge to set, and it slices and tastes best after 1–2 days. Keep it covered and eat it within 3 days.
- **To serve:** A small glass of Vin Santo or a strong espresso alongside is the classic pairing.

## Allergens

- **Egg:** raw yolks and whites in the cream.
- **Milk:** mascarpone; some dark chocolate also contains milk, so check the label.
- **Gluten:** Savoiardi biscuits. Substitute gluten-free ladyfingers if needed.
