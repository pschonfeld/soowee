  - title: Make the coffee
    text:
      - Brew {400 ml} strong coffee or espresso, pour it into a shallow dish and let it cool completely while you make the cream.
  - title: Make the cream
    text:
      - Separate {6} **eggs**, putting the whites in one large, clean bowl and the yolks in another.
      - Add a pinch of **salt** to the whites and whisk to stiff peaks.
      - tip: Whip the whites first. The same beaters can then go straight into the yolks without washing; the other way round, a trace of yolk fat would stop the whites from whipping.
      - Whisk the yolks with {6 tbsp} **sugar** for {timer:4-5m}, until pale, thick and falling from the beaters in a ribbon.
      - Add {750 g} **mascarpone** and beat on low speed just until smooth.
      - tip: Stop as soon as the mascarpone is smooth. Overbeaten, it turns grainy and can split.
      - Fold in the whites in three additions, cutting and turning gently to keep the air in, until no streaks remain.
  - title: Assemble
    text:
      - Dip {18} **Savoiardi biscuits**, one at a time, 1–2 seconds a side in the cooled coffee, just enough to soak through without turning soggy, and lay them in a single, snug layer in the dish.
      - tip: The biscuits decide the dish. Pick one that half of them cover in a single snug layer, and trim them to fit if needed.
      - Spread half of the cream over the biscuits and smooth it to the edges.
      - Dust with {15 g} **cocoa powder** through a fine sieve and grate {10 g} **dark chocolate** over it.
      - Dip the remaining {18} biscuits the same way, lay them on top and spread over the rest of the cream.
  - title: Chill and serve
    text:
      - Cover and refrigerate overnight, for at least {chill:8h}. It is at its best after 1–2 days.
      - Just before serving, dust the top with {15 g} **cocoa powder** through a fine sieve. Slice and serve chilled, with a little **icing sugar** if you like.
      - tip: Dust the top only when serving. Cocoa sifted on before chilling soaks up moisture from the cream and turns dark and patchy.
      - tip: The eggs are raw. For pregnant, elderly, very young or immunocompromised guests, use pasteurised eggs.

## Chef’s Notes

- **Make ahead:** Tiramisu needs the night in the fridge to set, and it slices and tastes best after 1–2 days. Keep it covered and eat it within 3 days.
- **To serve:** A small glass of Vin Santo or a strong espresso alongside is the classic pairing.

## Allergens

- **Egg:** raw yolks and whites in the cream.
- **Milk:** mascarpone; some dark chocolate also contains milk, so check the label.
- **Gluten:** Savoiardi biscuits. Substitute gluten-free ladyfingers if needed.
